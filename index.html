About Us

Beauty Begins With the Right Transformation.

At Scissorscut Makeover Studio in Model Town, Ludhiana, we believe a great look starts with understanding you. Your hair, your features and your personality all shape what suits you best, which is why every visit is about a style that feels like your own.

We focus on attentive, personal service: listening to what you want, talking through the options, and taking care over the finishing touches. Whether you are after a fresh cut, a change of colour or a complete makeover, we want you to leave feeling confident.
